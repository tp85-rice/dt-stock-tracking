## Candidates from the TSLA thread (not yet approved: move a line up to "Approved rules" to adopt it, or delete it)

- Registrations, permits and authorizations are not operating units; never count them as deployment. Origin: TSLA, Texas robotaxi registrations vs. cars actually driving.
- A metric the company withholds on a natural disclosure occasion is a signal: no data for one cycle, red after it is skipped. Origin: TSLA, Optimus unit count missing on four calls.
- Social-media and tracker claims get the if-true / if-false analysis and a dated falsification test, but never move the rating on their own. Origin: TSLA, nextbigfuture fleet claim.
- Footprint metrics (cities, units built, announcements) lag; validation metrics (failure rates, unit economics) lead. Find the one that gates scaling. Origin: TSLA, crash rate per mile vs. city count.
- When the company redefines a metric, break the series and say so rather than comparing across the change. Origin: TSLA, FSD subscriptions redefined in Q3 2026.
